Wake works on a sleeping/off computer with Wake-on-LAN enabled in its BIOS/settings, and needs this bridge running on another always-on device (here, this Mac). A Mac cannot wake itself once fully off — use "Keep this Mac awake" instead.

Setup 1) Fire TV: Settings → My Fire TV → Developer Options → ADB debugging ON. 2) On a computer on your WiFi: brew install android-platform-tools, then bun bridge/server.ts. 3) Paste the bridge address and the TV's IP (Settings → Network) above. Phone on https needs an https bridge address (e.g. cloudflared tunnel --url http://localhost:8788), or open the bridge's LAN address directly.
